Foundational Design Decisions
Anchor client selection rationale: ClaraVis provides a credible enterprise context for all three major AI use case categories (commercial/sales, financial/compliance, operations/IoT) in a single company, enabling cross-module narrative continuity. Medical device regulatory context (EU AI Act Annex III, ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR 820) raises the design bar appropriately for a FAANG-level portfolio.
GCP as the sole cloud platform: simplifies infrastructure design, maximises native integration (ADK, CCAI, Vertex AI, BigQuery), and produces a coherent IAM and VPC-SC story. Multi-cloud architecture was considered and rejected — the added complexity does not serve the portfolio's demonstration objectives.
Static GitHub Pages deployment: the portfolio IS the project — deploying it as a live site makes every design decision immediately verifiable. No build step, no framework, no backend — just HTML at repo root.
ClaraVis as a fictional company: avoids NDA, competitive intelligence, and client confidentiality constraints while allowing the portfolio to contain realistic data, contracts, payment amounts, and regulatory citations. All numbers are illustrative of a plausible enterprise at the described scale.
